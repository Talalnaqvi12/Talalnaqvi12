## Hi, I'm Talal 👋

I build Shopify storefronts for a living — everything from custom theme development to full platform migrations to squeezing extra milliseconds out of a slow homepage. Most of my work sits at the intersection of Liquid, JavaScript, and whatever backend glue (Python, Flask, APIs) a project needs to actually work the way a merchant wants it to.

I like the parts of Shopify development that go beyond swapping colors in the theme editor — turning a Figma file into a real section, untangling a messy metafield structure, or figuring out why LCP is sitting at 4 seconds when it shouldn't be.

---

## What I actually do

**Custom Shopify themes** — built section-by-section from scratch or heavily customized on top of existing themes, with an emphasis on code that a merchant (or the next developer) can actually maintain.

**Performance work** — LCP, INP, and CLS aren't just Lighthouse trivia to me. I look at preload strategy, image delivery, and how much JS is actually blocking the page.

**Platform migrations** — moving stores off WooCommerce or BigCommerce onto Shopify without losing SEO rankings, product data, or customer history in the process.

**Custom features** — product configurators, AJAX carts, dynamic UI, anything that Shopify doesn't hand you out of the box.

**Growth-side technical work** — GA4, GTM, and Klaviyo setups that actually feed useful data back to the merchant.
